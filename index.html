Waa'ee Keenya / Who We Are

INA Life & Wealth Academy (INA LWA) is a practical online academy focused on transforming lives through mindset, financial literacy, relationships, and digital skills.

Nuuf barnoota online, training live, mentorship, certificate fi community tokkummaa ni qopheessina. Kaayyoon keenya: of-amantaa, beekumsa maallaqaa, jaalala fi galii online guddisuu.
