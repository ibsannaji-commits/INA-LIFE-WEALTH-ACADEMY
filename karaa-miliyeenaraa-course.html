Curriculum Guutuu

Torban 1–32 — lesson tokkoon tokkoon

KUTAA 1

Bu'uura Maallaqaa (Torban 1–4)

01

Maallaqni Maali? Qabeenyi Maali?

Hiika, amaloota, gosoota qabeenyaa, Asset vs Liability, Net Worth formula, Miliyeenara vs Ofiin Miliyeenara

02

Galii fi Baasii

Active / Passive / Portfolio income, Fixed vs Variable expenses, Savings Rate, Lifestyle Inflation, Cash Flow

03

Baajata Maallaqaa

Zero-based budget, 50/30/20, Pay Yourself First, tracking systems, monthly review

04

Emergency Fund & Qusannaa

3–6 months fund, automatic saving, goal-based saving, sinking funds

KUTAA 2–3

Liqii, Qusannaa fi Qabeenya (Torban 5–8)

05

Liqii Gaarii fi Gadhee

Good debt vs bad debt, Avalanche & Snowball methods, credit score, debt freedom plan

06

Qusannaa Advanced

Savings rate 20%+, automation, compounding, delayed gratification

07

Galii Dabalataa Jalqabuu

Side hustle ideas, freelancing, skill monetization, first extra income

08

Qabeenya Ijaaruu Bu'uura

Productive vs Non-productive assets, first asset checklist, net worth tracking

KUTAA 4

Investiment (Torban 9–12)

09

Stock Market

Shares, Fundamental & Technical analysis, Dividend, Capital Gain, Diversification

10

Real Estate

Rental income, Cap Rate, Mortgage, Leverage, REITs

11

Bonds, Mutual Funds & ETF

Bond yield, Mutual funds, Index funds, ETF, expense ratio

12

Crypto & Digital Assets

Blockchain, Bitcoin/Ethereum, Wallet, Risk management (5–10% rule)

KUTAA 5

Daldala & Entrepreneur (Torban 13–16)

13

Business Models

Business Model Canvas, Revenue streams, Unit economics (CAC, LTV)

14

Marketing & Sales

4Ps, Digital marketing, Sales funnel, Brand building

15

Operations & Management

Supply chain, Inventory, Quality control, KPIs & OKRs

16

Scaling & Exit

Growth strategy (Ansoff), Exit strategies, Valuation

KUTAA 6

Psychology & Personal Development (Torban 17–20)

17

Money Psychology

Money beliefs, emotional spending, abundance vs scarcity mindset

18

Discipline & Habits

Habit Loop, Atomic Habits, Habit stacking, Environment design

19

Time Management & Productivity

Eisenhower Matrix, Time blocking, Pomodoro, Deep Work

20

Health & Relationships

Health–Wealth link, stress management, money communication with family

KUTAA 7

Advanced Wealth Building (Torban 21–24)

21

Tax Planning

Tax optimization, deductions, tax-deferred accounts, legal vs illegal

Sole prop, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, Trust, Insurance

Will, Trust, Inheritance, Life/Health/Property insurance
